Key Things to Note
SEO ≠ AIEO ≠ GEO — They Require Different Actions
Traditional SEO improves Google rankings through content and links. AIEO trains AI models to know and recommend Zithelo — requires Wikipedia, Wikidata, and press coverage that AI training data includes. GEO gets Zithelo into AI answer boxes — requires rendered FAQ content and articles that crawlers can extract.
All Developer Work Is Done. Only Team Actions Remain.
All schema, metadata, pages, tools, and structured data are now live — 11 new routes added in Sprint 2. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are trained on web data that is months old. Actions taken today will not appear in AI model knowledge immediately. Starting now matters — results compound over the 6–18 months before the next major model training cycle includes Zithelo's data.
